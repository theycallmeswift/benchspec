    How these numbers are counted

    Pass rate pools only the checks every arm runs, so arms are compared on the same lines. A check scoped to one arm counts in that arm's matrix cells, not here.

    Every other row covers graded samples: those that passed, failed or timed out. Errored samples are left out, so n can differ per arm.

    benchmark.md holds the final figures and their noise bands.
